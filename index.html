About

What I work on

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at Premier University, Chittagong. My thesis work sits at the intersection of NLP and low-resource languages — training transformer ensembles to recognize emotion across Bangla, Banglish (Romanized Bangla), and naturally code-mixed text, where most existing models fall apart.

Alongside research, I build full-stack web applications — from student platforms to event tools — using the MERN stack. I like projects where the model and the interface both have to work, not just one of them.

Currently exploring graduate research opportunities abroad, while finishing up my thesis and a related conference submission.
